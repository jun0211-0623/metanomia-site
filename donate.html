Why Your Support Matters

Support is how the research stays independent.

We publish in the open, and no sponsor decides what gets written. That independence holds only while the funding comes from readers who want the research to exist, rather than from parties who want it to reach a particular conclusion.

Your support pays for the work behind each report: the months of reading, the data, the review, and the editing that turns a question into a conclusion worth acting on.

It also funds what surrounds the research. The podcast, the Korean and English editions, and the education that makes these ideas legible to people meeting them for the first time.

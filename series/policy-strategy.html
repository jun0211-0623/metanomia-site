Policy & Strategy

What should we change or build, and which option should we pursue, and how?

Policy & Strategy reports move from analysis to action. They weigh the realistic options for changing or building something, and make the case for a specific path forward.

These reports are written for decision-makers, laying out not just what could be done, but what should be done, and the concrete steps to get there.

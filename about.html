About Metanomia

Metanomia is a research community that explores changes in the monetary and financial order and the crypto ecosystem, and turns its findings into clear, accessible writing.

Members Contact

Metanomia’s research is led by graduate student researchers in Hanyang University’s Department of Bitcoin and Monetary Philosophy.

Each report is developed through collaboration between its author and the research team. The author first prepares an outline, which the team reviews to refine the direction. The resulting draft undergoes written review and a report-specific review meeting, after which the author revises the manuscript in response to the feedback.

Once the review is complete, an editorial committee conducts a final assessment of the report’s structure, reasoning, factual accuracy, language, and format. Revisions are made in consultation with the author, preserving the author’s perspective and voice while strengthening the report’s quality and reliability. After editorial review, the research lead conducts a final review and approves the report for publication.

Only reports that meet Metanomia’s internal publication standards through this process are released.

Report Series

OUTLOOK
Forecasts the direction of change and how it may unfold.
FINDINGS
Analyzes evidence and cases to reveal how a phenomenon is structured and how it works.
PERSPECTIVES
Offers new viewpoints and questions for interpreting a phenomenon.
POLICY & STRATEGY
Proposes policy and strategic options for responding to institutional and market change.
GUIDE
Turns concepts and procedures into practical guidance for judgment and action.

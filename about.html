About Metanomia

Metanomia is a research community that explores changes in the monetary and financial order and the crypto ecosystem, and turns its findings into clear, accessible writing.

Core Team Contributing Researchers Contact

Metanomia’s research is led primarily by graduate student researchers enrolled in Hanyang University’s Department of Bitcoin and Monetary Philosophy. Each report is assigned an author and reviewers. The author first prepares an outline, which the group reviews to refine the direction, then develops and shares a draft with the reviewers. After written review and discussion at a regular meeting, the author revises the report to reflect the feedback and completes it once it meets the publication standard.

Metanomia publishes reports analyzing major changes in the monetary and financial order and the crypto ecosystem each month.

Each report identifies the evidence and sources underlying its judgments, and all reports are published free of charge.

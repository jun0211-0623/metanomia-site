About Metanomia

Metanomia is a research community that explores changes in the monetary and financial order and the crypto ecosystem, and communicates its findings in language anyone can understand.

Our Reports Core Team Contributing Researchers Contact

Metanomia’s research is led primarily by graduate student researchers enrolled in Hanyang University’s Department of Bitcoin and Monetary Philosophy. For each topic, authors and reviewers work together to refine the question, examine the evidence, and test the argument before completing the report.

Each month, Metanomia publishes reports analyzing major changes in the monetary and financial order and the crypto ecosystem. Reports are grouped into five series according to the nature of the conclusions they present: OUTLOOK, FINDINGS, PERSPECTIVES, POLICY & STRATEGY, and GUIDE. Each report clearly identifies the evidence and sources underlying its judgments. All reports are published free of charge.

Five Series

Reports are divided into five series by the kind of conclusion they deliver.

Outlook
A judgment on what is likely to happen next.
Findings
How a technology or market structure actually behaves.
Perspectives
Competing readings of a single question, set side by side.
Policy & Strategy
Where rules and regulation are heading, and what to prepare for.
Guide
Step-by-step instruction on how something is actually done.

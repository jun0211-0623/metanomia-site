Who We Are

Metanomia is an independent research institute that studies how the order of money is changing and translates its findings into language anyone can read.

Our Reports Our Team Our Fellows Contact

What We Do

Metanomia publishes monthly Crypto Trend Reports.

Each report goes beneath the surface of market events, tracing how a technology or market structure actually behaves before conclusions are drawn. Our work spans five series, from forward-looking Outlook to practical Guides, each defined by the kind of conclusion it delivers.

As crypto moves from the margins into the core of the financial system, the need for clear analysis has never been greater. We connect researchers, practitioners, and institutions to trace how monetary systems evolve, and turn that research into frameworks people can act on. Every report is published without a paywall, and the evidence sits in the text.

Five Series

Reports are divided into five series by the kind of conclusion they deliver.

Research

Perspectives

Policy & Strategy
Where rules and regulation are heading, and what to prepare for.
Guide
Step-by-step instruction on how something is actually done.

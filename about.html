About Metanomia

We are a research institute dedicated to understanding how money and value are being rebuilt.

Metanomia publishes monthly Crypto Trend Reports that go beneath the surface of market events, tracing how a technology or market structure actually behaves before conclusions are drawn. Our work spans five series, from forward-looking Outlook to practical Guides, each defined by the kind of conclusion it delivers.

As crypto moves from the margins into the core of the financial system, the need for clear, non-partisan analysis has never been greater. We connect researchers, practitioners, and institutions to study how monetary systems evolve, and translate that research into frameworks that lead to better judgment. Our work is independent, empirical, and open.

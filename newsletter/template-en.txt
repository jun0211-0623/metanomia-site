[How to use]

Keep the [EN] tag in the subject. It is what routes the issue to English subscribers.

Do not write the institute blurb or unsubscribe notice in the body. Both are added automatically on send.

Do not paste images into the draft. Link to images hosted on the site instead.

For an issue with more to say, add headings as needed. There is no need to fix a structure in advance.

Privacy Policy

Effective 18 August 2026

Metanomia processes only the minimum personal information needed to send email updates when new reports and videos are published. We do not operate user accounts, and you may use all public content without requesting email updates.

1. Information We Process and How We Receive It

The email update form on this site processes the following information.

  • Information you enter: your email address
  • Information sent with your request: language setting (Korean or English) and the page from which the request was made
  • Information generated while operating the service: request time, receiving status, and delivery or bounce records

We do not request your name, phone number, or payment information for email updates. Technical information that may be processed when you visit the site is described in section 6.

2. Purposes of Processing

  • Sending email updates about newly published reports and videos
  • Preventing duplicate requests and managing receiving status
  • Handling delivery failures and bounced addresses

We do not use email addresses for advertising or reader profiling.

3. Retention and Deletion

We retain the relevant information while you receive email updates. The “Stop receiving updates” link at the bottom of an email stops future delivery immediately.

After delivery is stopped, we may retain the email address and its stopped status until the service ends to prevent accidental re-delivery. To request complete deletion of the record, contact the address in section 9.

When the purpose of retention ends or a deletion request is confirmed, electronic records are deleted without delay using methods designed to prevent recovery. Information that must be retained by law is kept separately for the required period and then deleted.

4. Disclosure to Third Parties

Metanomia does not sell personal information and does not disclose it to third parties unless required by law or separately authorized by the individual.

5. Service Providers and International Transfer

We use the providers below to deliver email updates and operate the website. Information necessary for these services may be processed outside Korea as part of performing the services requested by the user.

Google LLC Purpose: storing email update requests and sending updates through Google Sheets, Apps Script, and Gmail
Country: United States · Data: email address, language, request page and time, receiving status, delivery and bounce records · Timing and method: encrypted network transfer when a request is made and an email is sent · Retention: as described in section 3
Vercel Inc. Purpose: website hosting, security, and incident response
Country: United States (processing may occur in other regions as the service operates) · Data: IP address, request time and path, browser and device information · Timing and method: encrypted network transfer when the site is accessed · Retention: the log retention period associated with the Vercel plan and configuration

You may avoid the international transfer associated with email updates by not entering an email address or by using the “Stop receiving updates” link in an email. This prevents email updates but does not affect access to public website content. You may avoid processing of technical access data by not using the site; without that data, the website may not be delivered.

6. Automatically Processed Data and Cookies

Metanomia does not use visitor analytics cookies, browser storage, or a separate analytics tool.

When pages, fonts, and static files are delivered, hosting and content providers such as Vercel, Google Fonts, and jsDelivr may technically process IP addresses, request times and paths, and browser or device information. Metanomia does not combine this information into separate user profiles.

7. Your Rights and How to Exercise Them

You may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or suspension of processing of your personal information at any time.

  • To stop receiving email updates: use the “Stop receiving updates” link at the bottom of an email. It takes effect immediately.
  • For other requests: write to the address in section 9. After any necessary identity checks, we will respond without delay and inform you of the outcome.

8. Security Measures

  • Accounts and personnel with access to personal information are limited to those who need it.
  • The website and email update request are encrypted in transit over HTTPS.
  • Emails are sent individually so that one recipient’s address is not exposed to another.
  • Access controls and security features offered by our service providers are applied, and related accounts are managed securely.

9. Privacy Contact

For questions, rights requests, complaints, or remedies concerning personal information, contact:

Emaillibertas.hson@gmail.com

You may also report a privacy concern to, or seek advice from, the following organizations in Korea:

  • Privacy Infringement Report Centre (118, privacy.kisa.or.kr)
  • Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (1833-6972, kopico.go.kr)

10. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will post the changes and their effective date on this page. Changes that materially affect users will be announced in advance where practicable.

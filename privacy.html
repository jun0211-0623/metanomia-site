Privacy Policy

Effective 27 July 2026

Metanomia collects email addresses in order to send report and video updates. We collect personal information for no other purpose, and browsing this site requires no information at all. This policy states plainly what we receive and how we handle it.

1. What We Collect and How

We collect the following through the email update form on this site.

  • Information you enter: your email address
  • Information recorded automatically with your signup: the time of signup, the page address you signed up from, your language setting (Korean or English), and your subscription status
  • Information recorded when we send an issue: the time of sending, the email subject, and the recipient address

We collect personal information through no other route. There are no user accounts, and we do not ask for your name, phone number, or payment details.

2. Purpose of Use

  • Sending report and video updates
  • Preventing duplicate subscriptions
  • Handling delivery failures and bounced addresses

We do not use this information for advertising or for profiling readers.

3. Retention and Deletion

We keep your address for as long as your subscription is active. The unsubscribe link at the bottom of every issue removes you from the send list immediately.

After you unsubscribe we retain your address in an unsubscribed state so that no further issue is sent to you by mistake. If you want the record deleted outright, write to us at the address below and we will delete it without delay.

4. Disclosure to Third Parties

We do not disclose collected personal information to third parties. We do not sell or share it.

5. Processing by Others and International Transfer

We rely on the following providers to operate the service, which means personal information is stored outside Korea.

Google LLC Storing subscriber records and sending email updates (Google Sheets, Apps Script, Gmail)
Country: United States · Data: email address, signup time, signup page, language, subscription status, send records · Retention: as stated in section 3
Vercel Inc. Website hosting
Country: United States · Data: IP address, browser information · Retention: per the provider’s own log retention policy

6. Cookies and Automatically Collected Data

This site does not use cookies. It uses no browser storage and has no visitor analytics installed.

Displaying the site does transmit your IP address and browser information to our hosting provider (Vercel) and our web font provider (Google Fonts). That is the technical communication required to render the page, and Metanomia neither collects nor stores it separately.

7. Your Rights

You may at any time ask to see, correct, or delete your personal information, or to have its processing stopped.

  • To unsubscribe: use the link at the bottom of any issue. It takes effect immediately.
  • For anything else: write to the contact below. We will act without delay and tell you the outcome.

8. Security Measures

  • Access to subscriber records is limited to the smallest possible number of accounts.
  • The site and the signup request are encrypted in transit over HTTPS.
  • Each issue is sent individually to one recipient, so no subscriber’s address is exposed to another.

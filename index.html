Metanomia publishes reports analyzing major changes in the monetary and financial order and the crypto ecosystem each month.

Reports are grouped by the kind of conclusion they present: OUTLOOK, FINDINGS, PERSPECTIVES, POLICY & STRATEGY, and GUIDE. All reports are published free of charge.

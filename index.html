Metanomia educates policymakers and the broader public about the changing nature of money through rigorous, non-partisan research and advocacy.

We convene researchers, practitioners, and institutions to study how monetary systems evolve, and to translate that research into clear frameworks that inform better decisions. Our work is independent, empirical, and open.

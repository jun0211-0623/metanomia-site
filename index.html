Report Series

Metanomia organizes its reports into five series, defined by the kind of conclusion each one delivers.

OUTLOOK Foresight

What is changing, why now, and where it is most likely headed within a defined horizon.

 RESEARCH Analysis

How and why a phenomenon works, and what conclusion the evidence actually supports.

 PERSPECTIVES Framing

What existing explanations miss, and the new frame we need to see it through.

 POLICY & STRATEGY Direction

What to change or build, and which option to pursue, and how.

 GUIDE Practice

Who does what, in what order and under what conditions, and how to respond when it fails.
